# Industry Archetypes & Style Tokens

This reference provides pre-configured design tokens, color systems, typography pairings, and storytelling pacing for the 4 core high-end industries. Use these archetypes as starting templates when generating new landing pages.

---

## Archetype 1: Haute Horlogerie & High Jewelry (Đồng hồ & Trang sức Xa xỉ)

**Vibe**: Precision mechanical mastery, astronomical time, golden age horology, quiet wealth.

### Design Tokens
```css
:root {
  --bg-primary: #0a0908;
  --bg-surface: #141210;
  --accent-gold: #c5a059;
  --accent-platinum: #d8dad3;
  --text-pure: #fbfbfa;
  --text-muted: #8c887b;
  --border-line: rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2);

  --font-serif: 'Cormorant Garamond', Georgia, serif;
  --font-display: 'Cinzel', serif;
  --font-mono: 'Space Mono', monospace;
}
```

### Canvas Scroll Narrative: "The Escapement Heartbeat"
- **Stage 1 (0–25%)**: The Tourbillon Cage at rest; macro-shot of rubies and hand-beveled bridges.
- **Stage 2 (25–60%)**: Escapement oscillation begins; balance spring breathing at 28,800 vph.
- **Stage 3 (60–85%)**: Skeletonized gear train meshing; astronomical moon phase disc advancing.
- **Stage 4 (85–100%)**: Full case assembly locked in 18k Rose Gold with sapphire crystal dome.

### Micro-Copy Tone
- *"Time measured not in seconds, but in astronomical revolutions."*
- *"Manufacture Calibre 1042 · Hand-beveled in Vallée de Joux · 72-hour power reserve."*
